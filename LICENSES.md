# Credits and licenses

Destinned bundles the following data, code, fonts and images.

## Code and astronomical data

- **Astronomy Engine 2.1.19** by Don Cross: planetary, lunar and solar positions. MIT License. https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy
- **Asteroid positions** (Chiron, Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta), 1800–2100: a compressed table generated with the **Swiss Ephemeris** by Astrodienst AG. The house systems were also checked against it.
  - The Swiss Ephemeris is dual-licensed: under the GNU AGPL v3, or under the Swiss Ephemeris Professional License from Astrodienst.
  - Using the site yourself, or sharing it freely with the AGPL's conditions met (offering the source, which here means `index.html` itself), is covered by the AGPL.
  - A commercial or closed-source deployment needs the professional license from Astrodienst. https://www.astro.com/swisseph/
- **Fixed star positions** are from the Swiss Ephemeris star catalogue (`sefstars.txt`), under the same terms as above.
- **Uranian (Hamburg School) points** use the orbital elements of Witte and Sieggrün as refined by James Neely, as published in the Swiss Ephemeris file `seorbel.txt`, under the same terms as above.
- **Asteroid 6583 Destinn:** computed by numerical integration from orbital elements published in the JPL Small-Body Database, a US government work.

## Places and maps

- **City gazetteer:** countries-states-cities-database by Darshan Gada (dr5hn), under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database
- **World map:** Natural Earth 1:50m land, public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com
- **Time zones and daylight saving history** come from your browser's built-in IANA time zone database.

## Images

- **Rider–Waite–Smith tarot**: Pamela Colman Smith's illustrations, first published by William Rider & Son in 1909, are in the public domain. The scans came from the metabismuth/tarot-json collection, resized for the web.
